Hi you! 🤗

How’s it going?

Today, I have again an excellent news you! 🚀

v15.1.8 has just been released!
This new version fixes several problems such as the issue with banned email addresses when generating fake profiles (ticket <a href="https://github.com/pH7Software/pH7-Social-Dating-CMS/issues/596" rel="noopener noreferrer">#596</a>), caused by <a href="/ph7builder-15-1-7-released/">v15.1.7</a>.

It also improves the <a href="https://php.net/manual/book.image.php" rel="noopener noreferrer">GD</a> Image processing class and now preserves the transparency color when uploading PNG images (ticket <a href="https://github.com/pH7Software/pH7-Social-Dating-CMS/issues/601" rel="noopener noreferrer">#601</a>).

Finally, pH7Builder 15.1.8 has all third-party libraries updated and the GeoIP database updated to its latest version.

Full changelog can be found here: <a href="https://github.com/pH7Software/pH7-Social-Dating-CMS/compare/15.1.7...15.1.8" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://github.com/pH7Software/pH7-Social-Dating-CMS/compare/15.1.7...15.1.8</a>

=&gt; Enjoy your Social Dating Website!!! 😍
