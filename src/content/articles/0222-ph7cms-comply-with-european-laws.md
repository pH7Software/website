If you are European, you probably know that in Europe there are many tiresome laws which doesn't exist elsewhere. However, we are concerned about this and we don't want to give more work to people living in one of the European Union countries.

So be it, the next version of pH7CMS (1.15) will provide a <strong>Cookie Consent Bar</strong> option to comply with EU laws. Obviously, this will be optional and disableable via the administration panel.

<strong>pH7CMS</strong> will be <strong>the first CMS to offer such a feature</strong> to comply with these laws.
