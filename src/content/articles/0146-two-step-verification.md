Hurray guys! Wonderful news today! ;)

Like how it was the case (and still the case!), with <a href="/dating-software-provider-to-accept-bitcoin/">Bitcoin Payment Gateway</a>, we are now again the first <strong>Social &amp; Dating Software Provider</strong> having integrated the <strong>Two-Factor Authentication </strong> (2FA) on pH7CMS. This great feature is available from <a href="/ph7cms-1-4-released/">pH7CMS 1.4</a> (currently only available with an TOTP mobile apps such as <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Authenticator" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Authenticator</a> or <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/2stp-authenticator/id954311670" rel="noopener noreferrer">2STP Authenticator</a>) and it will get better and better on the next releases.

Available for the Admins (of course, the most important!), Users and Affiliates!

This will give you the best and optimal security and good reputation for your online website!
