Hello all!

I am really happy today to announce you that the next pH7CMS version (1.2 branch) will integrate a new dating template (which will replace the current default template). This template will use the <a href="http://getbootstrap.com" rel="noopener noreferrer">Bootstrap</a> framework and will also be responsive in order to be suitable in all modern device like PC/Laptop, tablet/iPad, SmartPhone, iPhone, Android phone, etc.

The template will also be more focus on the "dating" niche and a little bit less on the social networking in order to best meet the needs of our customers.

I'll post screenshots of the new dating template on the blog as soon as possible.

 

Stay tuned!
