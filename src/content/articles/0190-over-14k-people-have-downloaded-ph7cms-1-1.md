Hello to all!

Today I am so happy to share with you some statistics of pH7CMS.

<strong>pH7CMS 1.1</strong> has met a wonderful success! The N°1 <strong>Open Source Dating Builder</strong> in its 1.1 version has been downloaded <strong>by over 14,000 people</strong> only on <a href="http://sourceforge.net/projects/ph7socialdating/" rel="noopener noreferrer">SourceForge</a> and in its Classic version (the Premium one is not included). The previous version (pH7CMS 1.0) was already a great success with over 13,900 downloads.

Now that pH7CMS 1.2 Beta version has been released (and now by default on Sourceforge), I am already very curious to know the next stats. We all know that "dating site builder software" is not used by everyone (even not a lot), however the next target is to reach over 30K downloads for pH7CMS 1.2. Anyway, the future will show the answer...

Again, hope you enjoy it and stay tuned!

See you soon,
Pierre-Henry, the founder.
