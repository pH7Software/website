Hi guys!

Today I have an excellent news for you!
It's now official! pH7CMS 6.0 will bring a brand new template. At this time, pH7CMS offers three professional and responsive templates by default. From pH7CMS 6.0, you will get one more for free :)

The new theme will be "<strong>Social Cartoon Niche</strong>". Ideal for young/teen social networks (pH7CMS already offers a "game" and "blog" modules which will match perfectly with the "Cartoon" theme).

Finally, the theme will be also 100% compatible and optimized for iOS devices (iPhone, iPad) and most of the Android devices.

See you very soon ;)

Stay tuned and be with us! :D

All the best,
Pierre
