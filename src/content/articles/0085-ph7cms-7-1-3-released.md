Hi all!

Today I'm again VERT VERY excited to announce the NEW RELEASE of pH7CMS! ;)

You can <strong><a href="https://sourceforge.net/projects/ph7socialdating/files/latest/download" rel="noopener noreferrer">download pH7CMS 7.1.3 here</a></strong> ;)
This new version is mainly a minor version after the major one which was 7.0.0.

This new version fixes a few minor bugs and typos and adds an additional security layer to prevent against reverse tabnabbing phishing attacks (when users add external links on their profiles), and a few improvements have been added as well :)

pH7CMS is now also translated into Spanish (in addition to French and Dutch!).

As you may know, pH7CMS doesn't include the translations by default (since most folks just need English), but you can add it to your site from the <a href="https://github.com/pH7Software/pH7-Internationalization&#x27;&gt;Internationalization repo&lt;/a&gt;.
