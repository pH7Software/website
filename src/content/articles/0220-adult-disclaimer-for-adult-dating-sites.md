As we have already done for the <a href="/ph7cms-comply-with-european-laws/">cookie warning bar</a> about the use of cookies.

Well, by continuing to help webmasters and let them save time and money, we have also decided to add an option to activate a "warning message" for adult sites and so protect minors. In many countries, this kind of disclaimer for adult sites is required by the law.
