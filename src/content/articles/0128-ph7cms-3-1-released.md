Hi folks!

Happy news again for you!! pH7CMS 3.1 has just been released (a new version made with lot of ❤️❤️❤️ especially for YOU!).

As you might know, pH7CMS uses now the <a href="/new-versioning-system/">new versioning system</a> since <a href="/ph7cms-3-released/">pH7CMS 3</a> and so this version had to be "3.1".
<h3>What's up in pH7CMS 3.1?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Possibility to change in the admin panel the number of profiles displayed on the splash homepage.</li>
  <li>Added nicer buttons (better design) for "mail", "report", "admin module manager" and "users browse" modules.</li>
  <li>A few bugs fixed (very, very minor).</li>
<li>mail module got a much simpler design (better for small devices such as iPhone 4/5).</li>
<li>User browse pages have been slightly redesigned (as well as "field" module for its homepage).</li>
<li>forum module got slightly improvements and the template (HTML) source files got new indentation (much nicer for developers).</li>
  <li>A few other minor improvements (good for your site but you won't probably notice them... ?).</li>
</ul>

Okay! I hope you are still enjoying your <a href="/dating-startup-by-step/">online dating service</a> powered by pH7CMS! ?

Finally, for those who are already running pH7CMS 3.0, there is no need to upgrade to pH7CMS 3.1 (go spending your time on better things that makes a difference ?).

Have an amazing and lovely day! ?

Stay tuned and be with us!

See you!
