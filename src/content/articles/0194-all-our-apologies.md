Hello everybody!

Some of our customers who have a pH7CMS website under a monthly membership, has occurred a licensing issue this week. <strong>We really want again to apologies about that</strong>.

Indeed our license verification server has been under a <strong>DDOS attack</strong>.
We begin to solve the problem.
Finally, note that version 1.8 is the last version to proceed like that. All next releases (from the next 1.2 version) will have a new license verification system, such a problem like that won't be possible again.

P.S. For anyone who still have this kind of problem, please remove the following folder -&gt;
<pre>/_protected/data/pH7_cache/str/</pre>
