Hi again guys!

I just wanted to let you know pH7CMS 1.4.1 has just been released.

Basically, unlike <a href="/ph7cms-1-4-released/">pH7CMS 1.4.0</a>, this is just a maintenance version that fixes several but minor bugs found during the hunting bugs I made last week. This version has also a few improvements.

The whole Geo-Localization IP database has also been updated (that explains why the zip file is larger for this version).
pH7CMS 1.4.1 package will give you a more stale website and always better as usual! ;)

Hope you will enjoy it :) pH7CMS 1.4.2 will be released very soon and an upgrade script will be available for the <a href="/pro/">pH7CMS Pro</a> holders.

Stay tuned!
