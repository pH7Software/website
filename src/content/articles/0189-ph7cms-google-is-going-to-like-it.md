<p>As you may know, nowadays, having a website optimized for mobile phones and SmartPhones is very important because most and most people use them.</p>
<p>That's why Google prefers websites optimized for them and we understand that (as our goal is to provide a software highly optimized for SEO.</p>

<p>pH7CMS 1.2 propose now by default two Responsive designs, which means that your dating site will be displayed well in any devices.</p>

<p>If you have a custom design, you can check if your website is "mobile friendly" by doing the <a href="https://www.google.com/webmasters/tools/mobile-friendly/" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google's mobile friendly test</a> and it will tell you if your site passes or fails ...</p>
